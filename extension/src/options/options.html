What stays on this device

  • Everything is checked here, by a detection engine built into the extension. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • Real values replaced by placeholders are kept in memory for the tab you are in, so the AI’s answers can be turned back. They are gone when you close the tab.
  • The history keeps the site, the time, what happened and masks of what was found. Never the text.
  • If the Shadow Lens dashboard is installed on this computer, the same history entries go to it, and nowhere else.
